Toddle-Cation demo

A practice walkthrough. Nothing you do here is saved or requests your location.

1. Pick a feeling

😊 Happy😢 Sad😠 Mad

Ask the child to choose the face that matches how they feel. The choices appear in a different order each time the app opens.

Use Hear the question to play the question again when a device voice is available.

2. Pick a color

RedBlueYellow

Choose any color that feels right. Color choices also appear in a different order each time the app opens.

Select Back to feelings to change the feeling.

3. Add notes or skip

The device owner may add optional notes, or choose Skip notes and continue. Choose Back to colors to change the color.

Notes are included in saved check-ins and CSV exports.

4. Allow location to save

An adult can choose Allow location and save and approve the browser's location request. Location is required; if permission is denied or location is unavailable, the check-in is not saved. Choose Cancel to return without saving.

The feeling, color, notes, date and time, location, and browser/device information are stored in this browser on this device.

5. Download or review

After saving, Save and Download Check-In downloads a CSV with the date, time, mood, color, and notes. Close keeps the saved check-in on the device without downloading it.

Choose Saved check-ins to review, export, or delete records. The full export includes location and device information, so keep it private. Records do not sync between devices or browsers.
